H2

Multimodal planning by diffusion

partially accepted

Diffusion Policy generates diverse grasp trajectories, unlike the deterministic heuristic, and meets the score criterion. The two strict thresholds are not reached: sampling takes 118 ms (DDIM-25) against a 50 ms threshold, and 2 modes are detected against a target of 3. Since the thesis, a distilled one-step sampler closes the latency gap. Re-measured per pose, no model shows alternative grasp strategies, as expected from deterministic demonstrations.

Trajectory diversity (jerk RMS · standard deviation)

More spread = more multimodality. The heuristic generates no diversity.

Extension: text-to-CAD → simulation → pose → grasp

new · 100% local

Generates test parts from a language description (build123d), simulates them in CoppeliaSim with real physics, estimates their 6-DoF pose from actual sensor depth and executes the grasp — all on the Mac, with a gradient-based pose refiner on the integrated GPU (Apple MPS).
